# DriveLogger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

DriveLogger is a Mac app that catalogs files in folders and drives you choose. Its developer does not receive your catalog, filenames, file contents, or usage activity through the app. DriveLogger has no advertising, tracking, analytics service, or account system.

## Files and local storage

When you grant access to a folder or drive, DriveLogger stores a searchable catalog on your Mac. This includes file and folder names, paths, file sizes, modification dates, drive identifiers, drive names, labels, and drive details. It saves folder access permissions and preferences locally so it can reopen selected locations after relaunch. Drive capacity is used for display and is omitted from new catalog exports.

Duplicate verification can read file contents locally to compare hashes. File contents are not uploaded to the developer. If you choose to delete duplicate files, DriveLogger performs the requested file operation on your selected storage.

## Catalog exports and storage services

Catalog exports contain file and folder metadata and drive details, and may reveal personal information through names and paths. Exports do not contain your saved folder access permissions or the contents of indexed files. You choose where to save them and whether to share them. A destination managed by a cloud, backup, or network-storage provider is subject to that provider's practices. Older exports may include drive capacity.

## Retention and your choices

Catalog entries remain on your Mac until you remove them from the catalog or remove the app's stored data. Disconnecting a drive leaves its catalog available for offline search. Removing a catalog entry does not delete the original files. Copies you export or back up remain until you delete those copies. Permissions can be managed in macOS privacy settings where applicable.

## Website and support

Opening our policy or support links opens GitHub in your browser. GitHub handles website access and any information you choose to submit under its own privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/v66cds2qtr-png/DriveLogger-support/issues. Issues are public. Do not post catalogs, filenames, personal paths, or other sensitive information.
